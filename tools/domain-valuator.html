Domain scorecard

A transparent liquidity score for any domain. Every factor is shown, so you can see exactly why a name ranks where it does.

What the score measures

The score estimates liquidity: how many buyers are likely to want a name and how quickly it could sell. For numeric domains the biggest factor is length, because each extra digit multiplies supply by ten. Chip status (no 0 or 4), a final 8, repeating patterns and the extension adjust the score. For word domains, length, hyphens, digits and pronounceability matter most.

It is deliberately not a price. Prices depend on recent comparable sales and on whether a specific end user needs the name. For that, request a human appraisal.

Frequently asked questions

Why no dollar value?

Automated dollar figures are often wrong by an order of magnitude. A transparent score plus real comparables is more useful.
