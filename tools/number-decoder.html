What the properties mean

Prime factorisation writes the number as a product of primes; every whole number above 1 has exactly one. σ(n) is the sum of all divisors, and comparing σ(n) − n with n tells you whether a number is perfect (equal), abundant (greater) or deficient (less). φ(n), Euler's totient, counts the numbers below n that share no factor with it and is central to RSA encryption.

A Harshad number is divisible by the sum of its digits. The digital root is what you get by summing digits until one remains; it equals the remainder after dividing by 9. Base conversions show the same quantity written in binary, octal, hexadecimal and base 36.

Frequently asked questions

How large a number can I decode?

Up to 15 digits. That keeps every result exact in standard browser arithmetic.

Is my number stored?

No. All calculations happen in your browser.

What is a semiprime?

A number that is the product of exactly two primes, such as 36118 = 2 × 18,059.
