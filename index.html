Why 36118?

It is a real U.S. ZIP code with no residents, an even semiprime (2 × 18,059), 8D16 in hexadecimal, and a five-digit "chip" that ends in the luckiest digit in Chinese culture. One number, four different stories, which is exactly what this site is built to uncover.

Frequently asked questions

What is 36118.com?

A free number-intelligence site. Type any number to see its mathematical properties, its meaning as a postal code, its cultural associations and how it scores as a domain name.

Is 36118 a real ZIP code?

Yes. 36118 is a unique ZIP code in Montgomery, Alabama, assigned to an institution rather than to households, which is why it has no residential population.
