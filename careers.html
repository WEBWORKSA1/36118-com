Work with 36118.com

We are a small, remote team building the clearest place on the web to understand numbers. Work is freelance and paid per project, with ongoing work for people who do it well.

Open roles

  • Contributing writer. Explain numbers in business, culture, AI or geography in clear, sourced English. Paid per article.
  • Data researcher. Verify facts, build datasets on postal codes and number markets, and keep tools accurate.
  • Front-end developer. Build fast, accessible tools in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Video creator. Script and produce short explainers for YouTube and social.
  • Community and contest moderator. Run Number Hunt rounds, judge entries and answer readers.

What we look for

  • Accuracy first, and the habit of showing your sources.
  • Plain writing and clean code.
  • Reliability on deadlines.
