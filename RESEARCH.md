## Decision

**Build 36118.com as a "number intelligence" platform**, not a one-topic ZIP page.

Why: 36118 has no brand meaning, so a site about 36118 alone caps out at a few hundred visits. A tool hub that decodes *any* number captures four large, evergreen search families (number properties, ZIP/postal lookup, lucky-number meanings, domain value) and uses 36118 as the flagship example. Revenue stack:

1. **AdSense** on high-dwell tool pages and guides (calculator/ZIP sites are proven AdSense earners).
2. **Lead generation** for numeric-domain buying/selling/appraisal and naming strategy — high ticket ($2.5k–$100k+ deals), commission-based.
3. **Sponsorships** (tool, guide, contest, video, newsletter) and acquisition interest in the domain itself.
4. **Donations** with allocation (ops, talent, promotion, prizes).

Rejected alternatives: pure ZIP directory (data-heavy, commoditised, thin-content risk for AdSense); pure numerology site (lower RPM, weak lead value); domain marketplace only (tiny audience, no AdSense volume).

Features adopted: search-first hero (Wolfram/OEIS); one page computing 25+ number properties with neighbour links (numbersaplenty); ZIP pages with sources and map (zip-codes.com, zipwise); factor-by-factor domain score with no fake price plus a human-appraisal CTA directly under the result (Atom/GoDaddy pattern); FAQ + FAQPage schema on every tool and guide; preset donation amounts (Buy Me a Coffee); number of the day + email capture (numerology.com); labelled sponsorship rate card (dnjournal); "Link to us"/sharing via permalinks (`?n=`, `?zip=`, `?d=`); zero-filled thin pages avoided (zipcode.org anti-pattern).
