Buy, sell or appraise a number

Numeric domains, memorable phone numbers and short names change hands every day, and the gap between a good price and a bad one is often large. Tell us what you have or what you are looking for. A person reads every request.

What you get

  • Buyers: a shortlist that fits your budget, outreach to current owners and negotiation support.
  • Sellers: an honest liquidity view, comparable sales and, if you want it, introductions to qualified buyers.
  • Brands: a number strategy across domain, phone and pricing, including cultural fit for Chinese-speaking markets.
  • Sponsors and partners: placements on tools, guides, contests and the newsletter.

How it works

  1. Send the form. It takes about a minute.
  2. We reply within two business days with an initial view.
  3. If it makes sense, we agree scope and fees in writing before any work starts.

Transactions should always close through a licensed escrow service. We never ask you to send funds to an individual.

Frequently asked questions

Is the appraisal really free?

Yes. A human reviews your name and replies with a liquidity view and comparable sales. Brokerage, if you want it, is quoted separately.

How fast will you reply?

Within two business days, usually sooner.

Do you sell my details?

No. Your details are used only to answer your request. See the privacy policy.
